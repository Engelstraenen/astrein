## Gemeinsamer Block (an jeden Prompt anhängen)
Photorealistic website storyboard for a modern German carpentry and joinery company named ASTREIN. One single 16:9 image, a 3x3 grid of nine panels with thin dark gutters, small panel numbers 1-9 in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a real photograph-quality still (full-frame camera, natural light, true textures, subtle film grain), NOT an illustration, NOT a sketch, NOT CGI. Same people, same workshop and same colour world in all panels. Modern workshop reality: CNC nesting router with dust extraction hood, sliding panel saw (Formatkreissaege), edge banding machine, veneer press, green line laser, tablets, ear defenders, safety glasses, modern dark slim-fit workwear. Young diverse team (women and men, 19-40). NO clichés: NO carpenter hats, NO traditional guild clothing (no Zunft, no corduroy bell-bottoms), NO pipes, NO rustic folklore, NO hand-plane romance, NO folding rule as hero prop, NO dovetail-only nostalgia, NO wood puns. Every door and drawer front has at most ONE handle or is handleless. Any on-screen website UI is minimal, clean, modern; only the text given in quotes, spelled exactly, everything else as unreadable thin lines.
