## V1 Stamm (Startbild ASF1) · 6 s
ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME, no still moment. One continuous shot, no cuts. Morning mist drifts steadily across the timber yard from left to right while the camera makes one slow, smooth push-in toward the cut end of the massive oak log; the growth rings on the cut end grow larger in frame, a few dry leaves tumble across the wet ground, the low sunrise light slowly becomes warmer. The log, the plank stacks and the ground stay exactly the same, nothing new appears, no people. Photorealistic, true colors, subtle film grain. Avoid: cuts, text, morphing, extra logs, people.

## V2 Hobeln (Startbild ASF2) · 6 s
ACTION STARTS IMMEDIATELY IN THE VERY FIRST FRAME, no still moment. One continuous shot, no cuts. The carpenter in the dark forest green work jacket pushes the thick live-edge oak plank steadily forward into the green thickness planer with both hands; the plank slides into the machine at an even pace, a continuous stream of fine wood shavings bursts out to the left and sawdust swirls in the window light. Both of his hands stay on the plank the whole time, his face and clothes stay the same. The camera holds steady with a very slight push-in. Photorealistic, true colors, subtle film grain. Avoid: cuts, hands leaving the plank, morphing, extra people, text.

Exactly six clamps from start to end, no hands, no people, nothing new appears. Photorealistic, true colors, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain. Avoid: cuts, extra clamps, morphing, text.
